Cavendish Estate

Location Design • SRA Labs

Daytime

The Cavendish coastal estate is the ancestral home of Siena and Ash Cavendish, and the residence of Reed. Developed over centuries, the full property spans hundreds of acres and reflects the family's old-world wealth, history, and influence. Beyond the mansion and its formal cliffside grounds lie extensive service buildings, a private hangar, and a runway — all positioned outside this view.

The family observatory is attached to the right side of the mansion. Beneath the house, a concealed laboratory is carved into the rock, connected to a submarine bay at the base of the cliffs. At the far end of the grounds, on the coastal point, stands an ancient astrolabe structure. It predates everything else on the estate; the Cavendishes neither built it nor control it, and the instrument moves according to forces they do not fully understand.

Generation & Craft Midjourney · GPT Image 2 · Photoshop · Krita · Flux

On the build. The environment was developed through reference research, Midjourney exploration, GPT Image 2 iteration, Photoshop and Krita paintovers, and Flux lighting studies — each pass focused on scale, geography, narrative function, and cinematic readability. The daytime, sunset, and dusk treatments above share one composition; use the toggle to read the relight across the three lighting registers.

Design Evolution

The estate was built up in stages — establishing architecture and scale first, then extending the coastline, populating the grounds, and finally carving the hidden infrastructure into the cliff.
