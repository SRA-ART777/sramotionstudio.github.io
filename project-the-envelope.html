Testing Grok Imagine 1.5 — A Compressed One-Minute Character Exchange

A few clips in The Siphon showed Grok Imagine 1.5 handling character work well enough to ask a real question: could it take dialogue scenes off Seedance's plate and cut the cost, with Seedance still carrying the work Grok can't land yet — fight choreography, path-following, the heavier spatial work it'll attempt but not execute cleanly? This is that test. A tense exchange across a single table, played as leverage and counter-leverage, built to push naturalistic performance: delivery, timing, micro-expression, and the emotional turn of the scene — and whether that performance holds consistently across a full scene, not just one strong clip.
